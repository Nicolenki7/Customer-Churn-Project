# Telco Churn — Key insights

_Dataset: `telco_churn_feature_engineered_for_tableau.csv` · **7,043** customers · source: [`src/churn_pipeline.py`](../src/churn_pipeline.py)._

## Headline

- Overall churn rate: **26.5%** (1,869 churned).
- Early tenure (≤12 months) churn: **47.4%** vs **6.6%** past five years.
- Churned customers pay more (74.44 vs 61.27 per month) and stay less (18.0 vs 37.6 months).

## Highest-risk segments

- Contract: **Month-to-month** (42.7%) vs 11.3% annual and 2.8% two-year.
- Payment: **Electronic check** (45.3%).
- Product: **Fiber optic** (odds ratio 2.71).

## Model drivers (logistic coefficients)

| Attribute | Coefficient | Odds ratio |
|:--|--:|--:|
| Contract: two year | −1.36 | 0.26 |
| Tenure | −1.17 | 0.31 |
| Internet service: none | −1.12 | 0.33 |
| Internet service: fiber optic | +1.00 | 2.71 |
| Contract: one year | −0.72 | 0.49 |
| Total charges | +0.53 | 1.69 |
| Online security: yes | −0.39 | 0.68 |
| Streaming movies: yes | +0.37 | 1.45 |

Full table: [`outputs/churn_feature_importance.csv`](churn_feature_importance.csv). Model: AUC **0.846**, accuracy 0.750.

## Business takeaway

- Focus retention on **month-to-month + fiber + first year** customers: that is where the volume and the rate overlap.
- Longer contracts, tech support and online security associate with lower churn.
- Price alone does not explain the exit: the combination of a high bill and a short relationship does.
